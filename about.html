our mission

we exist to create a soft, welcoming space where anyone can explore what happiness means to them.


through everyday practices, playful discovery, shared experiences, and a personal archive of joy, we help people notice what feels good, build habits that support their well‑being, and feel accompanied along the way.

daybreak is a world where happiness is not a pressure or a performance, but a gentle practice shaped by curiosity, community, and self‑understanding.


by helping users collect the small moments that matter, we hope to make happiness accessible, comforting, and naturally woven into daily life, becoming a lived experience rather than a distant ideal.

our approach

sometimes you'll want direction, and other times you won't. daybreak meets you where you are with our two sections.

wander

explore through fun and playful activities. wander pages contain small interactions that allow you to bring a little more joy into your day.

mess hall

explore a feast of small, funny, tactile, and creative activities designed so that something here puts a smile on your face, no matter where you're starting from. no rules, no wrong answers, no skill required. just play.

start the madness

silly decisions

at its core, the page celebrates the idea that happiness can come from not taking yourself too seriously. using exaggerated and silly choices to temporarily disconnect from reality. encourages users to engage without the pressure of making the "right" decision.

make Silly Decisions

sticker maker

this is the fun part where the user gets to layer in personality with a little emoji, a doodle or a phrase. the stickers here are designed by the creator of the site, so every option already has a consistent look and feel. picking one should feel like choosing a mood, not making a decision.

upload any photo
seek

connect with others, yourself, and learn more about the journey to happiness. seek pages are spaces to reconnect, reset, and guide you back to where you want to be with intentional interactions.

between us

between us invites users to share short stories that capture little pockets of happiness in their lives, or moments they're still hoping to reach. each post becomes a tiny snapshot of a lived experience, shaped by the voice and identity the user chooses to share with.

confess to the screen

just for you

holding on is where the human experiences settle into something personal, protected, and intentionally kept. it is a place where the user can create their own world by choosing what they want to keep, create, and design.

design your world

selfless less

this page explores how people pleasing habits can quietly distance us from our own happiness. instead of presenting "happiness" as a big concept, the experience focuses on a small, relatable shift by recognizing when we say "i'm fine" when we're not.

learn to be a little selfless less

our mission

we exist to create a soft, welcoming space where anyone can explore what happiness means to them.


through everyday practices, playful discovery, shared experiences, and a personal archive of joy, we help people notice what feels good, build habits that support their well‑being, and feel accompanied along the way.

daybreak is a world where happiness is not a pressure or a performance, but a gentle practice shaped by curiosity, community, and self‑understanding.


by helping users collect the small moments that matter, we hope to make happiness accessible, comforting, and naturally woven into daily life, becoming a lived experience rather than a distant ideal.

welcome.

your journal, your way — no rules here.

This is a space just for you. Write what's heavy, what's light, what you're afraid to say out loud. Stick things down, tear them up, start again.

There's no wrong way to use a journal. The only rule is that you show up — and you already did that just by opening this page.

🌿
intro · 01

getting started

  1. ① Pick up a tool from the trays on either side.
  2. ② Tear off some washi tape — click any roll.
  3. ③ Drag a photo or sticky note onto the page.
  4. ④ Stamp or sticker — then move things around.
  5. ⑤ Write freely. It saves automatically.
remember: you are enough ♡
start · 02

the desk.

← tape — click a colored roll to pick tape style, then drag across the page ← photos — drag the polaroid stack onto any page ← sticky — drag the yellow pad to drop a note anywhere ← pen — just for show (for now 🙂) → stamps — click DATE, MOOD, or ★ then click the page → stickers — click an emoji, then click to place it → clips — decorative for now
guide · 03

the pages.

Spreads flip like a real notebook. Use the dots at the top, the arrow buttons in each corner, or just scroll.

Each page is a canvas. Tape strips stay where you put them. Photos, stickies, stamps and stickers all move freely once placed.
